A simply supported square plate under uniform load, and one question: as the slab gets thicker, who says what? SAP two thousand is the judge.
With a paper-thin plate, thickness over span of one thousandth, all three agree within seven tenths of a percent: there is no shear here and everyone solves the same problem.
At one twentieth they start to split: Hekatan and the OpenSees MITC4 stay within one percent, but the Kirchhoff element is already almost four percent low.
At one tenth the Kirchhoff element drops nine percent: it has no shear deformation, and for a thick slab that can no longer be ignored.
Finally, with the slab one fifth of the span, Kirchhoff loses twenty four percent, while Hekatan and OpenSees stay within two percent of the judge, with the same sign: the CSI thick plate is not a MITC4, and that is measurable.
