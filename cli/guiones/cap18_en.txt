We will draw two curved surfaces in Hekatan Struct using only the ribbon: a dome and the skin of the Allianz Arena.
I close the panels. In the Areas tab I set eight segments per arc and turn on Guide: the meridian is a guide that is deleted when used.
I press Front and SNAP, zoom with the wheel, and with Arc I click three times: five zero, three four and zero five. Measure gives five metres, the radius.
With Select, a two-click window picks the meridian.
In Areas I set sixteen sectors, press Revolve and click the axis: one hundred and twenty panels.
Supports: a thin window over the base and Fixed. It applies to the sixteen selected nodes.
3D view and Zoom Extents: the whole dome, on the five-metre sphere.
Now the Allianz: in plan, a window and the Delete key erase the dome. Four segments, radius five, and Fillets by two corners: twenty-six by twenty-four.
In elevation, twelve segments and a three-point Parabola: the belly bulges one metre at mid-height, as Measure confirms.
One window selects the outline and the profile; Sweep and one click at the centre: four hundred and eighty panels.
Allianz supports: a window over the base and Fixed.
In 3D, the finished skin: five hundred and twenty nodes. Against ETABS, zero point zero one one percent.
