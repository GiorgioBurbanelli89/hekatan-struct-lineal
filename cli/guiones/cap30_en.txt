Hekatan Struct, the interface. Part one: the window, the menus and the ribbon. Everything we name gets framed.
A note before we start: this window is under development and is updated constantly. This is the September 19 version; menus may move.
Top left, the file buttons: new, open, save, save as, undo and redo.
The Menu button goes back to the main menu: new model, existing model, or examples.
New, and under development: the Analysis menu. Run the static analysis, run the modal analysis with its animation, and stop the animation.
Also new and under development: the Design menu. Today it has slab design as in SAFE, by strips or by finite elements. More will come.
On the right, Tutorial: short how-to videos, one per step. Next to it, the light or dark theme.
On the left, Settings: what is drawn, and which result you see.
On the right, the model: the template and its parameters, tools, loads, export, and units.
In the middle, the viewer: the structure in 3D and the color scale. And below, the AI agent: ask it for a structure, and it models it.
The Draw button opens the ribbon, which is the quick access bar. The two side handles fold the panels away, so the whole ribbon shows.
On the ribbon, help comes first: the question mark. Tap a button, and it plays an animated example of how to use it.
Drawing: line, polyline, rectangle, circle, arc, parabola and cubic. Under each button is its keyboard shortcut.
Structure: column, wall, slab, revolve and sweep.
Support, point load, and distributed load.
The views: plan, front, side and 3D. And precision: snap, ortho and object snap.
These two arrows: the upper one folds the ribbon. The lower one is quick access: it adds any button or control from the panels to the ribbon.
Second row, modify: undo and redo; select, move, copy, replicate, offset, trim, extend, reshape, erase, measure and construction line.
And Grid: it creates axes, levels and columns at once, from the span and story boxes.
Remember: this is under development. The ribbon is moving to tabs and more tools are coming. This video is re-recorded with every change.
