Here is an example of how it works, in Hekatan Struct on the web: the same footing from Braja Das's example, with the column offset in both directions.
In the Soil group there is the "tensionless soil" checkbox. When on, each soil spring works only in compression.
In the command window, every area carries its spring: areaspring, the subgrade modulus and the word compression.
The result: the loaded corner presses 81.9 tonnes per square metre, and the opposite edge lifts with zero pressure.
If I switch the box off, the soil pulls: the linear analysis gives 76.7, 6.4 percent less.
I switch it back on. In the Design menu there is the footing report, and on every step an "Explain" button.
And the Tutor, which explains the calculation step by step: the springs, the plate and the solver passes.
