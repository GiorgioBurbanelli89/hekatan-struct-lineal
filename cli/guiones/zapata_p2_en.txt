Part two: the same footing in Hekatan Struct. Das example 6.10: 1.5 by 1.5 metres, 606 kilonewtons.
The analytical model: a plate mesh at mid-plane and a soil spring at every node.
Springs in tension are switched off: that corner lifts and does not press the soil.
Maximum pressure: 81.9 tonnes per square metre, at the loaded corner.
If we turn off "tensionless soil", the linear analysis gives 76.7: 6.4 percent less.
