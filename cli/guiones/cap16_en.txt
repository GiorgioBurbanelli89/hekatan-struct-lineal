In this tutorial we replicate a chapel as an analytic structure, using only the cursor and the drawing menu.
Clean canvas. We tell the arc to discretize into six straight lines, because ETABS does not allow curves.
The first frame: two columns and a semicircular arc through three points. Watch it appear: the arc becomes six bars.
We repeat the frame along the axes at three, six, nine and twelve.
We stitch the frames with longitudinal beams at every node of the arc, so each bay becomes a closed cell.
One click on fill all closed cells turns each cell into a zinc panel, a membrane area that follows the curve of the vault.
And this is the chapel as an analytic structure; we orbit to see it complete.
