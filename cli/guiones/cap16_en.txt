In this tutorial we replicate a chapel as an analytic structure, using only the cursor and the drawing menu.
Clean canvas, front plane, and we tell the arc to discretize into eight straight lines, because ETABS does not allow curves.
The first frame: two columns and a three-point arc. As it is drawn, the arc becomes eight bars.
We repeat the frame along the axes at three, six, nine and twelve.
We stitch the frames with longitudinal beams: the ridge on top and the eaves on the sides.
The zinc roof is drawn as area panels, a membrane between each pair of frames.
And this is the chapel as an analytic structure; we orbit to see it complete.
