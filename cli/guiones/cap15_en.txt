The IFC file viewer in Hekatan Struct.
We import the IFC and see the whole model in three dimensions.
The panel lists the model objects: we can hide or isolate each one.
The panel also slides: it minimizes or hides completely, so it never covers the other menus.
The ruler measures on the model and snaps to corners.
The cut is activated in the Cuts X, Y, Z panel: I raise the plane height with the slider and, with the mouse, I check the Cut Z box.
The building opens at that plane; we orbit slowly to see the levels inside.
