The IFC file viewer in Hekatan Struct, using only the ribbon.
I open the ribbon, IFC and sections tab, and Import: pick the file and the model shows in 3D. Zoom Extents centres it.
Objects opens the IFC list: each object can be hidden or isolated.
That list minimises or hides behind its door, and Objects brings it back.
In the Draw tab, Measure works on the model and snaps to its corners.
In IFC and sections I type the height in the Z box and press Cut Z: the building opens there.
Holding the wheel I orbit the model to see the floors inside.
